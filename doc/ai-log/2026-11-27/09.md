## Table of Contents

- [Summary](#summary)
- [Root Cause & Fix](#root-cause--fix)
- [Verification & Repo Sync](#verification--repo-sync)

## Summary

Fixed why browser-opened `.pbm` problems disappeared on page refresh: large `private-*.inp` / `private-*.cor` test files exceeded `localStorage`'s 5 MB quota and caused `localStorage.setItem()` to fail silently.

## Root Cause & Fix

- Updated `ingestPbmEntries()` and `saveLocalProblemsToStorage()` in [public/index.html](file:///Users/siminshoeibizadeh/mienne/Programming/dev/projects/scholarship-project-uni/jutge-private/projects/02-pedit-cli-and-viewer/problem-editor/pedit/public/index.html) to skip `private-*.inp` / `private-*.cor` and files larger than 256 KB (100 KB per file in `localStorage`), and to trim the oldest entry only if storage quota is ever reached.
- Opened `.pbm` problems now persist across refreshes and can be cleared anytime via the **Clear** button.
