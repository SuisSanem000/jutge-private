
\Problem{Forbidden words}

\Statement

Write a program that, given a number $n$,
prints all the words
that are a permutation of the first $n$ lowercase letters,
with one restriction:
there cannot be two letters $x$ and $y$
such that $y$~is immediately to the right of $x$ in the word
and $y$ is the letter following $x$ in the alphabet.


\Input

Input consists of a natural number $n$ between 1 and 9.


\Output

Print in order and one per line
all the words that satisfy the restriction.
