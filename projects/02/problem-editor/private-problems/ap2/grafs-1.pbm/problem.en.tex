
\Problem{Graphs (1)}

\Statement

Given a directed graph with $m$ vertices and $m$ arcs,
we wish to know if there is a directed path between two given vertices.

\Input

Input starts with
 $n$ i $m$.
Then follow $m$ pairs $u,v$, with $u \ne v$, indicating an arc from $u$ to $v$.
We have that $0\le u<n$ and $0\le v<n$ and there are no repeated arcs.
Then follows, a pair $x,y$
with $0\le x<n$ and $0\le y <n$.


\Output


Write \qq{yes} o \qq{no}
according to whether there is or not a path from $x$ to $y$.
