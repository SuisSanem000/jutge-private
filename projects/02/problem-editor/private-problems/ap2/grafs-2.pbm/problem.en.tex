
\Problem{Grafs (2)}

\Statement


Given a directed graph with $n$ vertices and $m$ arcs,
we wish to know if there is a directed path between two given vertices.


\Input



Input starts with the value of $n$ and the $n$ names of the vertices.
Then comes the value of $m$ and follow the $m$ arcs, made up by the name
of two vertices, without repetitions nor autoloops.
Then follows a pair of vertices $x,y$. You can assume that
$x$ and $y$ belong to the graph.


\Output



Write \qq{yes} o \qq{no}
according to whether there is or not a path from $x$ to $y$.
